Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Nested News' publishing tools are private, internal tools used only by Nested News to publish Nested News' own videos to Nested News' own Facebook Page, Instagram account, and TikTok account (@nested.news).

The tools are not offered to the public and are not used to access other people's Pages, Instagram accounts, or TikTok accounts.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the tools use Meta's APIs to upload Nested News videos as Reels on the Nested News Facebook Page and the nested.news Instagram account, and TikTok's Content Posting API to upload Nested News videos to the @nested.news TikTok account, and to confirm that those posts were published.

The tools collect no data from viewers, followers, or other users.

Use and storage

The tools store only their own OAuth tokens, privately on the owner's machine, and use them only to publish and verify Nested News' own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

Because the tools do not collect or store personal data from other users, there is no user data to delete on request.

The owner can delete stored OAuth tokens at any time by removing them from the owner's machine and by removing the tools' access in Meta Business and TikTok developer settings. To request deletion of a Nested News video or to ask a question about this policy, email the contact address below.
