Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Nested Reels Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by Nested News to publish Nested News' own videos to Nested News' own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

The app is not offered to the public and is not used to access other people's Pages or Instagram accounts.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses Meta's APIs to upload Nested News videos as Reels on the Nested News Facebook Page and the nested.news Instagram account, and to confirm that those posts were published.

The app collects no data from viewers, followers, or other users.

Use and storage

The app stores only its own Facebook Page access tokens, privately on the owner's machine, and uses them only to publish and verify Nested News' own Reels. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

Because the app does not collect or store personal data from other users, there is no user data to delete on request.

The owner can delete stored Page tokens at any time by removing them from the owner's machine and by removing the app's access in Meta Business settings. To request deletion of a Nested News Reel or to ask a question about this policy, email the contact address below.
